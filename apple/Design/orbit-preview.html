The orbit mark, moving

This is drawn live, the same way the app will draw it: as shapes, not a picture, so it stays sharp at any size. A ball of thirteen orbit lines around a soft core of light that powers them: it glows in the look's own color, beats gently, and feeds every moving circle through a thin beam with pulses of light travelling out along it. The ball turns, and nine small circles run along it, each in a direction picked at random and picked again every few seconds.

The orbit lands where the agent acts, spins for about a second, and fades. When it types, the orbit rides along with the text and leaves when the last letter is in. It never takes clicks, and a switch in Settings turns it off.

The app icon

The mark, still, on deep glass: the one place it has a background. Everywhere inside the app it is drawn bare. Shown at Dock size and at the two small sizes macOS uses in lists.
